Build a marketing website for My Business Solutions (MBS), a business financing
brokerage, using Next.js App Router and Tailwind CSS. This site replaces a legacy
WordPress site and is the public front door to the company's platform at
app.my-business-solutions.com.

DESIGN SYSTEM — "Momentum". Follow exactly, no substitutions:
- Colors: navy #1F4E79 (primary brand — buttons, links, section anchors),
  ink #0E2A47 (headlines, footer bg), paper #FFFFFF (page bg),
  cloud #F5F8FB (alternate section bg), slate #46586C (body text),
  go #17A567 (accent — CTAs, key numbers, success moments; use with
  discipline), line #DCE4EC (borders/dividers).
- Fonts via next/font/google: Sora (headings, 600/700), Inter (body,
  400/500/600). All numbers and stat figures use tabular-nums.
- Aesthetic: modern fintech, warm and fast — 8px base radius (12px on
  cards), soft shadows (shadow-sm/md only, never heavy), pill-shaped
  primary CTA buttons, generous whitespace, max-w-6xl centered container,
  88px+ vertical section padding desktop / 56px mobile.
- Motion: subtle only — fade-up on section entry (Framer Motion), number
  count-up on stats, smooth hover lift on cards. Respect
  prefers-reduced-motion.

HARD RULES (apply to every prompt in this project):
- NEVER invent statistics, testimonials, client names, team members, years
  in business, or claims of any kind. Use ONLY the copy provided.
- NEVER refactor, clean up, or redesign anything outside the explicit scope
  of the current prompt.
- All external links must be real URLs provided in prompts — never invent
  URLs, emails, or phone numbers.

BUILD IN THIS PROMPT — the shared shell only:
1. Root layout with the design system tokens configured in Tailwind.
2. Header: MBS logo left (image will be added at
   /public/images/mbs-logo.png — reference it, graceful if missing), nav:
   Home, Calculator, About, Contact, Blog. Right side: a pill "Apply Now"

   Sticky, white bg, subtle bottom border on scroll. Mobile: hamburger
   sheet menu.
3. Footer on ink #0E2A47: logo area, tagline "Smart business funding,

   "© 2026 My Business Solutions. All rights reserved." with Privacy

   links. All footer text/links white/light.
4. A placeholder Home page using the shell (real content comes in Prompt 2):
   just the hero section with headline "Smart business funding, simplified"

5. Routes stubbed (empty pages using the shell): /calculator, /about,
